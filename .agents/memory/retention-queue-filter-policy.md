---
name: Retention queue filter policy
description: Approved product choice for retention-register status selection.
---

Keep retention-register selection to one exact supported status at a time, with an unfiltered view available. A multi-status selector or combined urgent bucket needs a new product decision.

**Why:** The owner approved the exact-status approach after considering a multi-status selector. It keeps the review action explicit without introducing an ambiguous new grouping.

**How to apply:** Preserve that choice when extending retention navigation or lookup. A reminder may select its matching exact status, but do not silently broaden that selection to other statuses.
