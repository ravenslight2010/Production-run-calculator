# Staff account deletion policy

**Rule:** Staff account deletion remains authorized by `manage-staff` plus the last-enabled-manager guard; it does not compare the target's capabilities with the actor's. The owner explicitly chose to keep this behavior.

**Why:** The owner reviewed the risk that a constrained staff administrator may delete a higher-capability account when another enabled manager remains, and chose not to change the existing deletion policy in the approved targeted safeguards.

**How to apply:** Preserve this behavior in future role-safety work unless the owner explicitly revises the policy. Do not infer permission to delete, disable, reassign, or otherwise change any account from this policy decision.
