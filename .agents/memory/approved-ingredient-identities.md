description: Inventory-owner decisions for production ingredient identity consolidation.

The inventory owner approved these canonical production-ingredient labels:

- Diced Chicken (C&F - 001MPDC40 or House of Raeford - 28501)
- Bacon (C&F 001ANUB40 or Tri Meats TM3514U)
- IQF Cilantro
- Goat Cheese

Keep Spinach, Fresh Spinach (broken up), and other fresh/chopped Spinach
variants separate unless new source evidence and an inventory owner explicitly
approve a consolidation.

**Why:** The approved labels have source-backed duplicate identities, while the
Spinach terminology does not reliably prove one physical warehouse product.

**How to apply:** Import and catalog reconciliation may map approved retired
spellings to these targets. Never use text similarity alone, and never combine
warehouse products, lots, barcodes, conversions, balances, or ledger history.
