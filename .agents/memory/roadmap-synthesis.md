---
name: Roadmap synthesis
description: How to answer broad questions about what remains across imported plans and research.
---

When the owner asks what remains or what to do next across imported documents, research, and plans, synthesize those sources into a prioritized roadmap rather than returning only a task-board summary. Reconcile dated plan snapshots with current code, evidence, and task status; distinguish completed work, active work, unresolved risks, and gated ideas. When asked about future test or skill needs, map them to planned initiatives instead of only auditing current-app coverage, and avoid proposing tests already completed.

**Why:** The owner clarified that broad “what next” questions refer to the imported documents, research, and plans, not only current task records.

**How to apply:** Review the relevant roadmap and source materials, verify their status against current evidence, then recommend a practical sequence and identify stale or conflicting plan claims. For test/skill research, distinguish feature-level tests to add with future work from environment/release evidence gates, and reuse existing specialist guidance where it already fits.
