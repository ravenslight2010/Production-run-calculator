---
name: AI memory health audits
description: Safety boundary for reviewing confirmed AI corrections versus facility knowledge and private conversation history.
---

Confirmed correction memory can be checked and deterministically repaired against authoritative merge and learned import alias maps, but a source label’s absence from current master-data menus is never enough reason to remove it.

**Why:** Historic source wording can remain a valid lookup for incoming data after the product, brand, or ingredient was merged or renamed. Natural-language facility facts have context that deterministic matching cannot safely infer, and per-user conversations are private context rather than factory cleanup data.

**How to apply:** Keep correction audits read-only until explicit confirmation, only repair rows with authoritative duplicate/cycle/canonical-target evidence, and surface facility knowledge separately as review-only. Exclude conversation history from both reports and cleanup.
