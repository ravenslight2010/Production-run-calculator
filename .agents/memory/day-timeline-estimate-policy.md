---
name: Day timeline estimate policy
description: Distinguish actual run ends, packaging-clear projections, and unknown timing without inventing overlap or setup assumptions.
---

Treat a projected finish as packaging clear, not press completion. Preserve recorded
run-end timestamps as actual evidence even when product may still be draining.
Future runs are conservatively sequential through transit; actual overlapping runs
retain their real timestamps. Paused forecasts assume resume now and must say so.

**Why:** A run can be ended while product remains on the line. Mixing actual end
records with a press/WIP-subtracted live duration produced incomparable editor and
summary estimates. No staffing, setup/changeover allowance, or intentional overlap
model was approved as part of repairing those estimates.

**How to apply:** Share one estimate basis across schedule preview and Day timeline.
Do not turn unknown durations or unplaced breaks into zero or a confident whole-day
finish. Never change stored recipe inputs or authoritative progress to make a
projection look complete.

Timeline recipe inputs and packaging progress have different owners: draft cases,
pack and rate stay editable, while every live run's packaging counters come from
its own durable progress register, whether selected or not.

**Why:** Packaging can be accepted independently of a saved run-form snapshot.
Reading plausible but stale counters from that snapshot materially overstates work
remaining, even when the selected run and the pure duration calculation are correct.

**How to apply:** Reconcile progress after choosing recipe/draft inputs, do not
borrow counters from the selected run, and keep future-date planning independent.
Regressions should exercise the actual storage-to-view adapter with disagreeing
saved values and progress, not only a preassembled calculation object.
