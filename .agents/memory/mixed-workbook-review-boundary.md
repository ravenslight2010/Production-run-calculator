---
name: Mixed workbook review boundary
description: Owner-approved session-only grouping without cross-import transaction or retained workbook sources.
---

Related workbook groups are navigation-only tab-memory sessions. Each file opens
its existing importer and has a separate human Apply. Do not introduce Apply all,
group rollback, automatic repair or persisted raw workbook/group source evidence.

**Why:** The owner approved this bounded design because server-atomic imports,
local guide writes and independently acknowledged schedule days do not share a
transaction. Treating cancellation as rollback would misstate saved outcomes.

**How to apply:** Keep atomic uncertainty attached to the original review and
operation identity; distinguish partial schedule acknowledgements and local
verification from server delivery. New durable group retention or cross-device
handoff needs a separate product/retention decision, not an implicit queue change.
