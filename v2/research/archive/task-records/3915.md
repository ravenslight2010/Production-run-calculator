# Package V1 Research for V2

## What & Why
Create a portable, source-preserving research and planning bundle inside `v2/` so V2 can be moved to another Replit project without losing V1's relevant research, decisions, plans, or evidence. Review and reconcile the collected materials against current V1 evidence and approved V2 scope, and add targeted primary-source research only where existing materials leave a consequential gap.

## Done looks like
- `v2/research/` contains a portable README, source catalog, V1-to-V2 reconciliation, gap register, prioritized roadmap, and an archive of in-scope source documents and task-plan snapshots.
- Each archived item has its original location or task ref, date/revision where known, status/authority, provenance, and a content hash. Exact duplicates are recorded as aliases; non-identical revisions remain distinguishable.
- The V1 assessment reuses the existing app-wide research reconciliation rather than repeating it wholesale, and verifies material/current claims against the current V1 source, focused tests, and accessible task records.
- The gap register covers gaps material to V2's approved first release: run and station behavior, inventory, imports/master data, authentication/roles, multi-device sync/offline behavior, auto-tracking, cutover, and deployment boundaries. Existing V2 decisions and archived or draft task proposals are clearly distinguished.
- New research uses suitable primary sources, includes access dates and limits, and is added only when it can change a V2 decision or close a meaningful evidence gap.
- The bundle remains navigable when `v2/` is copied to a clean directory or another Replit project; it does not depend on files outside the bundle or on live task-system access to understand its conclusions.
- V1 remains unchanged; no production data, customer workbooks/recipe contents, credentials, sessions, or secrets are copied into the bundle.

## Agent-owned execution
- **Objective and owner:** The assigned research/documentation agent owns source discovery, safe archiving, reconciliation, gap research, synthesis, and bundle validation end to end. The objective is a portable V1 knowledge package for V2, not implementation of the V2 roadmap.
- **Affected surfaces:** New files under `v2/research/`; minimal link-only updates to `v2/README.md` and/or `v2/HANDOFF.md`. V1 application code, runtime, deployment, and data are read-only. Do not modify V2 application code or alter existing V2 implementation status.
- **Evidence and access:** Use `docs/research-reconciliation/` as a completed V1-wide inventory/audit input; inspect its source catalog, coverage ledger, matrix, and current overlay. Add relevant material from `research/`, `docs/`, `.agents/memory/`, filtered research/planning uploads in `attached_assets/`, relevant local source plans in `.local/tasks/`, and accessible project task records. Search current tasks for overlap and read full plans for material records. Task-plan snapshots must preserve ref/title/date/state when available; the prose bundle must not rely on task refs alone. Record the actual inspected V1 revision and dates for any evidence reused or refreshed. Hidden task conversations, inaccessible records, and unavailable production evidence are not assumed accessible.
- **Source-format and lifecycle matrix:** Inventory Markdown, plain text, JSON/index files, DOCX, PDF, and any other encountered research format before processing; use the uploaded-file routing guidance and record unreadable/scanned or truncated sources as gaps rather than silently omitting them. Preserve source language and note it in metadata; do not translate or normalize source claims without labeling the transformation. Classify every material item as owner-approved, implemented contract, active work, draft/proposal, archived/cancelled, experiment, external reference, or historical evidence. Treat filenames/dates as provenance clues, not approval. Do not ingest workbook contents, customer recipe/specification data, production records, or secrets; record excluded source classes and reasons.
- **User-controlled decisions or permissions:** None expected for the approved documentation-only scope. Do not request secrets, production access, or permission to copy excluded customer/operational data. If a source contains personal or sensitive information, do not archive it raw; record the limitation and accountable owner/next step.
- **Failure ledger:** Keep a single ledger for missing, unreadable, sensitive, conflicting, stale, unsupported, and not-currently-verifiable material. Each unresolved item must name evidence, status, reason, owner, and next action. Fix safe documentation/reference defects in this task; do not create follow-up tasks for source-reading or validation sub-outcomes.
- **Overlap and boundaries:** Task #3715 (Compare all research and plans with the current app) is merged and its outputs under `docs/research-reconciliation/` are available; reuse and refresh its applicable findings rather than duplicating its entire app-wide assessment. Task #3907 (Finalize V2 Architecture and Data Plan) is merged and is the existing V2 architecture source. Tasks #3908–#3912 are archived, while #3913 and #3914 are drafts; preserve these distinctions and do not treat archived plans as approved requirements or re-create their implementation tasks. This portable documentation outcome is distinct from existing V2 feature/data-transfer and authentication outcomes, so it has no task dependency.

## Applicable safeguards and verification
- Follow `task-execution-rules`, `documentation-claim-review`, `evidence-hygiene`, and `reading-uploaded-files`. Use `test-gap-triage` when identifying V1/V2 test gaps so recommendations point to the correct source-of-truth layer and do not duplicate completed coverage.
- Apply claim labels consistently: verified, partially verified, unsupported, stale-suspected, contradicted, or needs-human. Separate these evidence labels from lifecycle status. Bind code/test claims to the inspected revision; historical tests, browser evidence, and telemetry remain bounded to their reported revision, environment, and scope.
- Verify the archive manifest against copied files and hashes; report exact duplicates and near-duplicate/version families separately. Check every local link, ensure primary conclusions are understandable from within `v2/`, and test portability by copying the completed V2 research bundle to a temporary clean directory and opening/validating its internal references.
- Run a focused privacy/sensitivity pass over retained files and the final package. Do not retain raw credentials, personal data, operational records, customer workbook contents, or imported source rows. A hash is provenance, not sanitization.
- No application test suite, deployment/publish check, production query, database access, data migration, browser certification, or release GO/NO-GO is required. If a source or claim needs such evidence, mark it blocked/not run and identify its owner and next action instead of treating missing evidence as a pass.

## Out of scope
- Implementing features, tests, migrations, or the roadmap recommendations in V1 or V2.
- Copying V1 production data, customer workbooks/recipe contents, credentials, sessions, secrets, or unrelated binary artifacts.
- Creating or changing a Replit project, artifact, deployment, database, integrations, or publishing configuration.
- Rewriting historical source documents in place or treating all historical suggestions as current requirements.
- Re-running the full app-wide V1 audit or claiming production/device verification beyond the evidence available.

## Steps
1. **Inventory and scope sources** — Reuse the existing reconciliation inventory and coverage records, search relevant repository, local task-plan, and uploaded planning materials, and query accessible task records. Classify by domain, format, language, date/revision, authority, lifecycle, sensitivity, duplication, and availability; capture scope exclusions and unresolved access gaps.
2. **Build the portable source archive** — Copy in-scope source documents into a structured `v2/research/` archive without editing originals. Export relevant task plans as self-contained snapshots; retain original metadata and hashes, group exact duplicates without losing provenance, and record sensitive/unreadable items as bounded exclusions.
3. **Reconcile V1 findings and approved V2 scope** — Reuse Task #3715's report and Task #3907's architecture, then verify material current-state claims against current source/tests and task status. Map V1 capabilities and evidence limits to V2 launch requirements; add targeted, primary-source research for material gaps and explicitly preserve competing options and human-owned decisions.
4. **Publish the V2 research brief** — Add the portable index, source register, reconciliation matrix, gap/failure ledger, and prioritized roadmap with relative links. Update existing V2 overview documents only with links and a clear statement of the bundle's scope and evidence date.
5. **Validate portability and safety** — Recompute and compare hashes, validate manifest coverage and relative links in a temporary copied bundle, review status labels and source provenance, and scan retained content for excluded data or sensitive material. Record all remaining failures, blockers, and unknowns with owners and next actions.
